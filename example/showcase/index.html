About this data

The shared dataset is a loan-origination process — the journey a mortgage application takes from intake to funding. Cases enter at Application Submitted, pass through a credit check and document collection, get an initial review, and then route to underwriting. The clean path continues through property appraisal, conditional approval, clear-to-close, and ends in Loan Funded.

The interesting structure lives between Underwriting and Documents Received: when underwriting needs more from the applicant, the case loops back through Request Additional Documents → Documents Received and is re-underwritten — sometimes more than once. Cases that never recover end in Declined; cases the applicant abandons end in Withdrawn.

About half of cases take the happy path; the other half deviate through the loop or one of the terminal outcomes. Most cycle time piles up inside that loop. Switch the embed to Mean (time) mode to see the bottleneck light up amber, or pick a case in the trace panel to walk through one loan end-to-end.
